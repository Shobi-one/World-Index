## Profile

Jambo One is a robot who was used to hunt for a group of misfits. He gained sentience through a situation and now follows Ezekiel, "the only man he knows".

## Notes

- Believes feelings are a code made to control people  
- Protects the gang under Ezekiel's orders  
- Doesn't understand what he is  

## Background

After nearly being destroyed, something happened to Jambo's wiring, and he murdered his old family. All he remembers is they forced him to kill and farm to give them food, but never gave him any for a reason he couldn't understand.

Now, he works with Ezekiel, who gives him his spoils and treats him just like another group member and not a slave.
