## Profile

Father Azrael Dante Romano is a 34 year old Italian-American immigrant child of an imposing stature and size. Beneath a grizzled appearance, Father Azrael is a calm tired man who has spent his entire life in a smalltown as a member of its church. After his town was massacred and church burnt to the ground, Father Azrael has had his faith tested time and time again yet he shoulders this and his new found family's burdens: after all suffering is temporary and the Creator offers absolution.

## Notes

* Tired of life yet stays resolute.
* Offers guidance and shepherds his flock.
* Harbouring a dark secret from his past.

## Background

Azrael doesn't remember his life in Italy, all he knows - or rather been told by his family - is that his bloodline holds both a curse and a blessing, and this lead to them being driven out from their ancestral home. His family immigrated their way to the America's settling into the newly created Tontitown, Arkansas. Here is this rural setting is where Azrael was raised living there for 30 years. Since a kid Azrael had always been big (most likely from his bloodline) and this only continued as he got older. As such he's spent much of his childhood tending to their families farm and generally offering his physical labour to his extended family (which came along with his immediate family) and anyone within his town. Apart from his physical capabilities two things always captivated Azrael, the first was his love for wildlife, and the other his devotion to his faith. Whilst the former sprouted from his work on his farm, the latter came from his uncle. Father Dante, for where Azrael's middle name is derived, set up a small chapel in Tontitown and, once he was of age, Azrael joined the faith. Something within Azrael drew him so fully into pursuing the faith, something that tugged at his very soul and once he began his worship he felt at peace. This peace couldn't last. Maybe it was the curse his family held; maybe it was bad luck or maybe He had forsaken his followers. Whatever it was ruin came. The entirety of Tontitown was butchered, house burned and people ripped to shreds all by one thing: "Father" Gascoigne. Azrael was in the chapel when he heard the screams and mid worship at the altar he was found by Gascoigne. Yet something made him spare Azrael. It could of been a small mercy from the Lord or the voices in Gascoigne switched their tune, whatever it was Azrael was the only living soul of the Romano family. Azrael walked around, weeping as he saw the carnage and his home burning to the ground. Hate and vengeance filled his heart. Somehow the Lord had allowed this to happen, allowed his entire livelihood to be turned to ash and a pool of blood. 

Azrael took his Fathers gun, his Uncle's crucifix and rosemary beads and his Mothers horse - Dominic, a large chestnut coloured workhorse. He set out after the butcherer of his family, spending days in the wilds fighting through hunger, dehydration and exhaustion all so he could paint the grass with the crimson of this false priest. When finally Azrael found Gascoigne, he went to claim his vengeance. Azrael felt himself slipping into a rage quite unlike he had ever felt, one that he could not control. Just as he was about to fully slip into whatever it is that heightened his anger, the Lord filled him with not hate but remorse and mercy. Yet again, the Lord confounded Azrael, why should this monster be given mercy after the acts he committed? To this day Azrael's questions remained unanswered and his faith equally wavered. The mental and physical toil took Azrael and when he awoke he found himself in the care of The Graves Gang, his ailments being treated by Clara. Since then Father Azrael had stayed with the gang. Whilst he may not fully agree with the methods of The Graves Gang (their excessive violence and acceptance of "Father" Gascoigne), he is still a lost man and the leadership of Ezekiel draws him to stay. Also deep down Azrael pines for a family now that he has lost his, and over the months Azrael has begin to see them as just that, though they are all just a bit strange.
