## Profile

Clara Hartwell keeps the Graves Gang alive. She serves as the gang's medic, quartermaster, and moral compass. Practical, sharp-minded, and fiercely loyal, Clara is one of the few people willing to challenge Ezekiel Graves when his decisions put the gang at risk.

## Notes

- Treats wounds and manages the gang's supplies.
- Calm under pressure and difficult to intimidate.
- Values innocent lives and avoids unnecessary bloodshed.
- One of Ezekiel's most trusted confidantes.

## Background

Clara grew up in Dustfall, where she learned herbal medicine and practical healing from her mother. After local mine owners drove her family from their home, she drifted west and eventually crossed paths with Ezekiel Graves. He offered her a place in the gang, and she became the steady hand that holds the family together.
