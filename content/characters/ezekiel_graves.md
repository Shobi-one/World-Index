## Profile

Ezekiel Graves is the charismatic and calculating leader of the Graves Gang. Once a lawman, he abandoned the badge after corruption cost him his family. He now leads a band of outlaws bound by loyalty rather than blood.

To his gang, Ezekiel is a father figure and strategist. To the law, he is one of the most wanted men in the West.

## Notes

* Believes freedom is worth any price.
* Protects his gang like family.
* Haunted by the deaths of his wife and son.

## Background

After losing his family to powerful railroad interests, Ezekiel turned against the system he once served. Over the years, he built the Graves Gang from drifters, deserters, and lost souls.

Now as the frontier grows and the reach of the law, Ezekiel must decide how far he is willing to go to keep his family alive.
