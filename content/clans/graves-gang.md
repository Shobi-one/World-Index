## Overview

The Graves Gang is one of the most notorious outlaw bands in the West. Known for train robberies, jailbreaks, and daring escapes, they are bound together by loyalty rather than blood. To the law they are criminals, to each other, they are family.

## Traits

- Operates with military precision under Ezekiel Graves.
- Protects its own above all else.
- Leaves behind legends, rumors, and wanted posters.
