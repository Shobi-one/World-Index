## Overview

Red Mesa is a vast plateau of crimson stone rising above the western frontier. Feared by settlers and indigenous tribes alike, it is a place of strange disappearances, ghost stories. Few who venture too close return unchanged.

## Features

- Towering red sandstone cliffs and labyrinthine canyons.
- Frequent dust storms that distort sound and direction.
